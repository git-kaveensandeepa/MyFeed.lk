Commitment to User Privacy

At MyFeed.lk, we respect your right to control your personal data. In full compliance with Google Play Store policies and international data protection standards (GDPR / CCPA), users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

Method 1: Direct In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

If you have the MyFeed.lk app installed on your smartphone or tablet, you can delete your account immediately:

  1. Open the MyFeed.lk app.
  2. Tap the Profile / Account icon in the navigation bar to visit Account & Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Actions section.
  4. Tap "Delete Account & Data" and confirm your request.
  5. Your account credentials, profile, and all saved items are wiped permanently and immediately from our database.

Requests sent to our data protection team are verified and processed within 7 business days.

Data Deletion & Retention Policy

Data Permanently Deleted:
  • Firebase Authentication credentials
  • Full Name & Email Address
  • Profile Avatar & User Bio
  • Saved Bookmarks & Reading History
  • Quiz scores, points & leaderboards
  • All user comments & feedback
Retention & Backups:
  • Retention: Zero personal data is retained following deletion.
  • Timeline: In-app deletion is instant; web email requests are processed within 7 business days.
  • Backups: Temporary disaster recovery backups are purged automatically in 30-day rolling cycles.
