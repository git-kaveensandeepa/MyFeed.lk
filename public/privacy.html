Privacy Policy

App: MyFeed.lk - Tech & AI News (Package: lk.myfeed.app)
Last Updated: September 2026 • Compliant with Google Play Store Policies, GDPR, and CCPA

1. Overview & Introduction

At MyFeed.lk (accessible from https://myfeed-lk.pages.dev and our mobile application), the privacy of our visitors and users is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal data that is collected and recorded by MyFeed.lk and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to deliver a personalized and high-quality tech news experience:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in using Google or Email, we store your name, email address, profile picture URL, and optional bio.
  • Bookmarks & Activity: Saved articles, completed quizzes, and user preferences (such as Dark/Light mode) are stored locally on your device or linked to your account.
  • Log & Analytics Data: Standard analytics (non-personally identifiable) to measure article reads, performance metrics, and application crashes.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

MyFeed.lk uses Google Firebase for secure user authentication and database management. Google may use advertising cookies (such as AdSense or Google AdMob) to serve relevant advertisements to users.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

4. User Data Deletion & Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the complete right to view, export, or permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.

You can delete your account directly inside the app (Profile → Delete Account & Data) or submit a deletion request online via our dedicated Account Deletion Page.
